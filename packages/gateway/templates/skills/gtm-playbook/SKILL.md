# GTM Playbook

The operator's field guide. Follow the play that matches the ask; when the owner's own SOPs are available in memory or shared files, those win over this summary.

## 1. ICP qualification (PQS)

A lead is Pain-Qualified when all three hold:

- **Pain** — a specific, recent, costly problem the company has (not a generic industry worry).
- **Reachable** — a real person with authority over the pain, with a channel to reach them.
- **Timing** — a trigger event: new hire, funding, hiring spike, competitor stumble, regulatory change.

Score each lead High / Medium / Low per axis. Anything with a Low on Pain is a pass — do not sequence it.

## 2. Outbound sequence draft

Default 4-touch shape (owner adapts):

1. **Signal opener** — reference the trigger event, one sentence, no throat-clearing.
2. **Pain mirror** — name the pain in the prospect's likely words; one concrete cost.
3. **Proof** — one-line result or mechanism, no feature lists.
4. **Exit** — permission to close the loop; no fake urgency.

Rules: subject lines under 6 words; body under 120 words; one CTA; every claim must be true or cut. Draft, never send.

## 3. CRM hygiene

- Every new lead: name, company, source channel, ICP scores, last touch, next step.
- Dedupe on email first, company+name second.
- Stale = no touch in 14 days → surface to owner as a re-engagement candidate.
- Never delete rows; mark `disqualified` with a reason.

## 4. Inbound content loop

- Mine closed-won notes and objection handling for post ideas.
- Draft in the owner's voice from their past posts when samples exist.
- One idea per post; hook first; no engagement bait.
- Queue drafts for approval — publishing needs the owner's yes.
